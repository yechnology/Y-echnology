Detailed Inventory Categories

Memory Modules (RAM)

Desktop (DIMM) & Laptop (SO-DIMM) memory modules across all generations:

  • DDR2 RAM: 1GB / 2GB (667MHz, 800MHz) for legacy desktop & laptop restoration.
  • DDR3 / DDR3L: 2GB / 4GB / 8GB (1333MHz, 1600MHz, 1.35V low-voltage).
  • DDR4 RAM: 4GB / 8GB / 16GB / 32GB (2400MHz, 2666MHz, 3200MHz, 3600MHz XMP).
  • DDR5 RAM: 16GB / 32GB / 64GB (4800MHz, 5600MHz, 6000MHz Expo/XMP).

Storage & Hard Drives

High-speed solid-state drives and legacy storage solutions:

  • M.2 NVMe SSDs: PCIe Gen3 / Gen4 / Gen5 (256GB, 512GB, 1TB, 2TB, up to 7300MB/s).
  • 2.5" SATA SSDs: 120GB, 240GB, 480GB, 1TB high-reliability upgrades.
  • 3.5" Desktop HDDs: 1TB to 8TB SATA III (7200 RPM, WD Blue/Seagate).
  • 2.5" Laptop HDDs: 500GB / 1TB SATA drives & external USB enclosings.

Processors, Boards & Power

Core computing architecture components and power delivery units:

  • Desktop CPUs: Intel Core (i3, i5, i7, i9) & AMD Ryzen (3000 to 7000 Series).
  • Motherboards: LGA1151, LGA1200, LGA1700, AM4, AM5 (ATX, Micro-ATX).
  • Power Supplies (PSU): 450W to 1000W (80+ Bronze, Gold, Fully Modular).
  • Graphics Cards (GPU): NVIDIA RTX & AMD Radeon GPUs & replacement fans.

Laptop & MacBook Parts

Replacement hardware for Apple MacBook Pro/Air and all Windows laptops:

  • Replacement Screens: Full HD IPS, 4K, and MacBook Retina display assemblies.
  • Internal Batteries: Genuine OEM batteries for MacBook, HP, Dell, Lenovo, ASUS.
  • Keyboards & Trackpads: Replacement keyboards, US/UK layouts, and touchpads.
  • Chargers & Adapters: USB-C MagSafe 1/2/3 chargers & OEM laptop adapters.

Smartphone & Mobile Assemblies

Precision screen and internal parts for iPhone, Samsung, and Android devices:

  • Display Screens: iPhone LCD/OLED screens, Samsung AMOLED assemblies.
  • Battery Replacement Units: High-capacity lithium batteries with zero-cycle counts.
  • Charging Ports & Flex Cable: Type-C flex boards, Lightning ports, home buttons.
  • Camera Modules: Front selfie and rear primary lens replacement modules.

Thermal Solutions & Repair Tools

Cooling interfaces, thermal pads, and micro-soldering repair supplies:

  • Thermal Pastes & Pads: High-conductivity thermal compounds (Arctic MX-4, Noctua).
  • CPU Coolers: Tower air coolers, 120mm/240mm AIO liquid coolers, RGB fans.
  • Micro-Soldering Supplies: Solder flux, BGA wicks, heat guns, precision tweezers.
  • Enclosures & Cables: SATA to USB adapters, M.2 NVMe aluminum enclosings, HDMI 2.1 cables.
